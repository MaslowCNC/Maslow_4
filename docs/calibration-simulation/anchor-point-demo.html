Anchor Point Locating System - Geometric Approach

Concept: Given a first measurement point and distances to 4 anchors, we can directly compute the locus of possible anchor configurations. This is geometrically more accurate than searching along a diagonal and then an arc.

Method:
  1. Given: First waypoint location (x₀, y₀) and measured distances to 4 anchors: d_TL, d_TR, d_BL, d_BR
  2. Each anchor must lie on a circle centered at (x₀, y₀) with radius equal to its measured distance
  3. For a rectangular frame: TL and BL are on left edge (same X), TR and BR on right edge (same X)
  4. For a rectangular frame: TL and TR are on top edge (same Y), BL and BR on bottom edge (same Y)
  5. These constraints define a locus of valid rectangular configurations
